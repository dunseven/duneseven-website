Pricing questions.

Is the free version usable?

Yes. Energy modes, tasks, priorities, Today at a glance, unlimited habits, Journal, Capture, the Focus timer, the home screen widget, Week, Month, Reset my day and Help are all free with no card and no trial clock. Two things are metered on Free: Bookmark is three a month, I’m Stuck is three a month, and history goes back seven days.

What exactly does Pro add?

Five things. Unlimited Bookmark, repeating routines (My roster and Weekdays), Your Month in your energy colours with your repeating routines drawn on it, unlimited I’m Stuck, and your complete history. That is the whole list. Everything else stays free.

Is Lifetime really a single payment?

Yes. A$129.99 once gives you every Pro feature permanently, including features released in future versions. There is no renewal and no reminder email. The price rises to A$179.99 on 1 January 2027.

What happens to my data if I cancel Pro?

Nothing is deleted. Your entries stay yours. You return to the Free limits, seven days of visible history and three Bookmarks and three I’m Stuck a month, but everything you have written remains, and resubscribing opens it back up.

Can I switch from Pro to Lifetime?

Yes. Purchase Lifetime and your subscription can be cancelled through the App Store or Google Play. Manage both through your store account.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies, since that is where the purchase is made. Apple’s is through Report a Problem; Google’s is through the Play Store order history.
