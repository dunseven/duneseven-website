Pricing questions.

Is the free version genuinely usable?

Yes. Energy modes, tasks, priorities, unlimited habits, Journal, Capture, the Focus Countdown Timer, Week, Month and Reset my day are all free with no card and no trial clock. Two things are metered on Free: I’m Stuck is three a month, and history goes back seven days.

What exactly does Pro add?

Four things. Repeating routines (roster patterns and weekdays), the full Month with your Energy Story, unlimited I’m Stuck, and your complete history. That’s the whole list. Everything else stays free.

Is Lifetime really a single payment?

Yes. A$129.99 once gives you every Pro feature permanently, including features released in future versions. There’s no renewal and no reminder email. The price rises to A$179.99 on 1 January 2027.

What happens to my data if I cancel Pro?

Nothing is deleted. Your entries stay yours. You’d return to the Free limits, seven days of visible history and three I’m Stuck a month, but everything you’ve written remains, and resubscribing opens it back up.

Can I switch from Pro to Lifetime?

Yes. Purchase Lifetime and your subscription can be cancelled through the App Store or Google Play. Manage both through your store account.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies, since that’s where the purchase is made. Apple’s is through Report a Problem; Google’s is through the Play Store order history.
