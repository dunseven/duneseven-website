We lead from the front: honest, transparent, supportive, always. Dune Seven doesn’t ask you to bend to a system — it’s guided by you, and it flows with you, the way water finds its own shape around whatever’s in front of it. We use AI where it genuinely helps and nowhere else — you are always the one deciding, never the one being managed. Because you were never the thing that needed fixing — you have real feelings and real strengths, and here there’s room made for both, and space to channel them, not manage them away. We keep Dune Seven human.

Our approach

An AI planner that doesn’t decide for you

Plenty of apps now call themselves AI planners. Most of them mean an algorithm that rearranges your day and expects you to keep up. Dune Seven is the other kind. You choose your energy. You set what matters. Where AI appears at all — turning a task that has become a wall into three to five steps you can start — it supports the decision you already made. It never makes it for you.

Our story

A human-driven planner, built by someone who needed it

Dune Seven began in 2019 as a paper planner Angela Kocek made for herself — a serving firefighter of more than ten years, working out how to function while shift work, motherhood and her own moving capacity kept redrawing the week. It grew into an app the same way it started: one real problem at a time, guided by the people living them.

You know your energy. Room is made for it here.
