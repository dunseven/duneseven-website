The human-driven planner.

We lead from the front: honest, transparent, supportive, always. Dune Seven doesn’t ask you to bend to a system. It’s guided by you, and it flows with you.

Read on

We use AI where it helps and nowhere else. You are always the one deciding, never the one being managed. That’s what brings the walls down, one at a time: ADHD, neurodivergence, burnout, shift work, the postpartum fog. Not because we fixed you. You were never the thing that needed fixing. You have real feelings and real strengths, and here there is room made for both. We keep Dune Seven human.

Built by two parents on different sides of the world.

Dune Seven began in 2019 as a paper planner Angela Kocek made for herself, a serving firefighter of more than ten years, working out how to function while shift work, motherhood and her own moving capacity kept redrawing the week.

Read on

Building it began at home. We looked first here in Australia, and the lesson that came back wasn’t about development at all. It was about purpose. Dune Seven can’t be built as someone’s line item. It has to be built by someone who knows why it exists. So we kept searching until we found alignment, and we found more of it than we could have hoped for.

His name is Abdullah, and he builds every screen: a father on the other side of the world, working around his own family and his own competing priorities. Between us sits a time difference that leaves the tiniest window to talk in real time. It has never mattered once. We both know exactly what Dune Seven is for: to see you flourish.
