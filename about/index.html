The human-driven planner.

We lead from the front: honest, transparent, supportive, always. Dune Seven does not ask you to bend to a system. It is guided by you, and it flows with you.

Read on

We use AI where it helps and nowhere else. You are always the one deciding, never the one being managed. That is what brings the walls down, one at a time: ADHD, neurodivergence, burnout, shift work, the postpartum fog. Not because we fixed you. You were never the thing that needed fixing. You have real feelings and real strengths, and here there is room made for both. We keep Dune Seven human.

Built by two parents on different sides of the world.

Dune Seven began in 2019 as a paper planner Angela Kocek made for herself, an emergency services worker and Navy veteran, working out how to function while shift work, motherhood and her own moving capacity kept redrawing the week.

Read on

Building it began at home. We looked first here in Australia, and the lesson that came back was not about development at all. It was about purpose. Dune Seven cannot be built as someone’s line item. It has to be built by someone who knows why it exists. So we kept searching until we found alignment, and we found more of it than we could have hoped for.

His name is Abdullah, and he builds every screen: a father on the other side of the world, working around his own family and his own competing priorities. Between us sits a time difference that leaves the tiniest window to talk in real time. It has never mattered once. We both know exactly what Dune Seven is for: to keep it human, because you are.

Dune Seven is built in Angela’s personal capacity and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to her employer.

An AI planner that does not decide for you.

Plenty of apps now call themselves AI planners. Most mean an algorithm that rearranges your day and expects you to keep up. Dune Seven is the other kind.
