You know your energy. We just plan around it.

Pick Flow, Low or Focus and the whole app rebuilds around it. On a Low day it quiets down to what you can face. No wearable. No algorithm deciding for you.

Read on

Most planners assume unlimited energy, a perfect memory and a straight line through the day. If that isn’t you, Dune Seven is. Every task stays reachable in all three modes; what changes is how much is asking something of you right now.

We use AI only where it helps (I’m Stuck turns a wall of a task into steps you can start) and nowhere else. You are always the one deciding. We keep Dune Seven human.

Why we will never ask for a wearable

The shift worker planner

When a task becomes a wall.

Tap I’m Stuck and it turns into three to five steps you can actually start. On any task, on any day.

Read on

Task initiation is the wall, not the task. I’m Stuck exists for the moment you’ve been staring at the same line for twenty minutes. It never judges the task or the delay; it just breaks it down and hands you the first step. Three a month on Free, unlimited on Pro.
