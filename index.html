What's inside

Built around how you actually function

Flow, Low and Focus

Choose the energy mode that matches your capacity and Home rebuilds around it. Every task stays reachable in all three — what changes is how much screen you have to face. No other planner does this.

Today at a glance

Your timed things as calm blocks across a single scrolling strip, so you can see where your time actually goes and where the open space is. The answer to "where did the day go?"

habits, without pressure

water, movement, nutrition and read. Track what matters without guilt when life gets in the way. Streaks are a guide, not a scorecard.

Journal and Capture

Journal is a quiet, private space to unload your head. Capture is for the thought that arrives at the worst possible moment — put it down in two taps and sort it later.

Focus Countdown Timer

A visual depleting ring that makes time real — the single biggest help for time blindness. Start it, watch it empty, and finally feel how long things actually take.

I'm Stuck

Tap it on any task that has become a wall, and watch it turn into three to five steps you can actually start. Your personal coach, on call whenever the wall goes up.

Also inside: priorities, the Monthly Mantra, Week, Month, and Reset my day for when today needs to start over.

Questions

Before you start

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Dune Seven Planner is free to download and every feature in it is free to use right now. There's no subscription available to buy and nothing in the app is locked.

What is Pro, and when does it arrive?

Pro arrives at hard launch and adds exactly three things: unlimited I'm Stuck, the full Month with the Energy Mirror, and your complete history. Annual is $69.99 a year with 14 days free. Monthly is $12.99 a month, billed from day one, with no trial.

Will the features I use now be moved behind Pro?

No. Energy modes, tasks, priorities, habits, Journal, the Monthly Mantra, Capture, Week, the Month calendar and Reset my day are free and stay free. Pro only ever adds things that don't exist yet.

Do I need an ADHD diagnosis to use it?

No. Dune Seven was designed around executive dysfunction, time blindness and mental load, but it's built for anyone whose capacity changes day to day — shift workers, parents, and people recovering from burnout. The app doesn't diagnose anything, and it never claims to.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Dune Seven Planner is on the App Store for iOS and on Google Play for Android.
