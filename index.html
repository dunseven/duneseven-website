Built by someone who needed it
“The Post-it notes were getting too expensive. So I built an app instead.”Angela Kocek, founder

Dune Seven began in 2019 as a paper planner Angela made for herself in Mildura, Victoria, working out how to function when her own capacity kept moving. An emergency services worker, a Navy veteran and a mother, she has lived on a roster her whole working life. The first attempt to build the app cost $47,000 and delivered lessons instead of a product. She did not stop.
