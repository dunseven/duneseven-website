You know your energy. We plan around it.

Pick Flow, Low or Focus and the whole app rebuilds around that choice. On a Low day the app quiets down to what you can face. No wearable. No algorithm deciding for you.

Read on

Most planners assume unlimited energy, a perfect memory and a straight line through the day. If that is not you, Dune Seven is. Every task stays reachable in all three modes. What changes is how much is asking something of you right now.

We use AI only where it helps. I’m Stuck turns a wall of a task into steps you can start, and nowhere else does AI appear. You are always the one deciding. We keep Dune Seven human.

Why we will never ask for a wearable

Your week has never looked like a week.

Four on, four off. Nights. FIFO swings. Two days, two nights, three off. Set My roster once and your repeating routines travel with it, block after block.

Every other planner offers “daily” or “every Monday”. Neither is your life. Everything inside, and what it costs

Built by someone who needed it.

“The Post-it notes were getting too expensive. So I built an app instead.”Angela Kocek, founder

Dune Seven began in 2019 as a paper planner Angela made for herself in Mildura, Victoria, working out how to function when her own capacity kept moving.

Read on

An emergency services worker, a Navy veteran and a mother, Angela has lived on a roster her whole working life. Every feature exists because a real executive-function problem needed solving at her own kitchen bench, usually at 5am, usually between shifts.

The first attempt to build the app cost $47,000 and delivered lessons instead of a product. She did not stop. The second attempt is the one on your phone.

Dune Seven is built in Angela’s personal capacity and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to her employer.
